How it works

1
Install this app — you've already done this part.
2
Get in touch at info@inova.technology to activate a subscription — you'll receive a Tenant ID and connection secret for your company.
3
Enter your credentials under Settings › InovaConnect › Configuration.
4
Open InovaConnect from the menu — your team is in.
What to expect: this app is a lightweight connector only — it doesn't store chat history and does nothing on its own without an active InovaConnect subscription. Every conversation, AI feature and message log lives on Inova's hosted platform, kept fully separate from your Odoo database.
